# Animations

When a number of points between 100 (included) and 1000 (excluded) are awarded, a small star (Star.png scaled down to a width of 20px) is sent from the last tile played to the score counter per 100 points awarded (3 stars for 300 points and so on). A delay of 50ms separates each star sent.

When 1000 or more points are awared, a medium star (Star.png scaled down to a width of 50px) is sent per 1000 points awared. Also with a small delay between each of them.
